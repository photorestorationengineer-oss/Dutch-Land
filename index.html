FAQ

Questions about learning Dutch?

Is this suitable for complete beginners? +

Yes. The A1 section starts with basic Dutch greetings, introductions, vocabulary and simple everyday conversations.

What is covered at A1? +

A1 focuses on basic communication such as introducing yourself, family, food, shopping, home, numbers, time and daily routines.

What is covered at A2? +

A2 introduces longer conversations and situations involving travel, work, hobbies, past experiences and plans.

Are translations included? +

The dialogue format can show Dutch sentences together with clear English translations to help beginners understand the meaning.
